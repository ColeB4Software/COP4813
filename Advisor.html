This advising rogram will allow students to drag and drop courses that they need for their program of study.
- To start off, drag one course you will be taking or have finished into completed courses or semester column.
- Drag your course to the left if you have already taken them
- Drag your course to the right if you want to add it to your schedule
- You can't add your prerequisites alongside your main course in the same semester
- To take one of your clases out of either the completed course or Semester plan, press the Undo button in the white box above.

Courses in green are draggable, showing their prerequisites have already been met.
Press the Undo button to remove the last course added to either the completed courses or semester plan.
Courses in yellow cannot be dragged because their prerequisites have not yet been yet.
