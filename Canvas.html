The equations below are used to calculate the coordinates needed to create a spirograph drawing on the canvas. Enter the values for the variables and press theDraw Spirograph button below to create the pattern.


x = (R+r)*cos(t) - (r+0)*cos(((R+r)/r)*t)
y = (R+r)*sin(t) - (r+0)*sin(((R+r)/r)*t)
R = the radius of the outer (fixed) circle
r = the radius of the rotating (inner) circle
O = the offset of the pen from the center of the inner circle
